\section{Exploratory Topic Model}
\label{app:exploratory-topic-model}

\input{output/tables/topic_models/descriptive_topic_manuscript_values}

The selected descriptive topic model began with a country-year-stratified sample of
\DescriptiveTopicSourceSampleN{} articles. GPT-5.1 produced language-neutral
English abstractions of their political-corruption content;
\DescriptiveTopicBoundaryN{} boundary or unclear cases were retained for audit
but excluded from clustering. BERTopic assigned
\DescriptiveTopicInlierN{} of the \DescriptiveTopicUsableN{} usable
abstractions to \DescriptiveTopicN{} direct topics and treated
\DescriptiveTopicOutlierN{} (\DescriptiveTopicOutlierShare\%) as outliers.
The selected density specification achieved a mean resample adjusted Rand
index of \DescriptiveTopicMeanResampleARI{} across the five stability checks.

Table~\ref{tab:pc-descriptive-topics} reports the weighted distribution of the
direct descriptive topics. These are exploratory summaries of recurring
coverage, not validated corruption-type measures or inferential outcomes.
